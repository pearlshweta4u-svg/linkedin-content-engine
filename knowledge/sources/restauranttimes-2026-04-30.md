These are notes on the published interview: facts and short verbatim quotes only, not the full article.

## Facts
- Entrepreneurial journey started in 2012 with a chain of preschools in Bangalore.
- Founded PretiumPro, an HR consultancy, where she came across Lo! Foods.
- Joined Lo! Foods in 2019 as an HR consultant (initial hiring), moved into sales, and eventually headed the entire B2C channel.
- Under her leadership, Lo! Foods built 20+ cloud kitchens across four cities, reaching nearly 18,000 people. She left in 2022.
- Lo! Foods built its own subscription model after customers complained about delivery-platform fees, absent riders and no advance booking. Its kitchens could run "in literally 100 square feet of space" by partnering with underused kitchens.
- **Own health story:** after her second pregnancy, while managing a demanding career, she gained significant weight and had hormonal disruption. She tried almost everything; nothing worked. Once she understood her physiology, she **lost over 20 kg**.
- Clients: mostly high-functioning professionals and mothers who have already tried calorie deficits, clean eating, fasting, supplements and willpower-based plans.
- Five pillars: blood sugar and insulin regulation; strength training as a metabolic and hormonal tool; gut health and inflammation reduction; stress and sleep support; sustainable nutrition for real life.
- Also works with women using or considering GLP-1 medications (muscle preservation, nutrient sufficiency, long-term independence).
- Interview line: "Clients often lose 7–9 kg in 90 days." This is her own claim, not independently verified. Don't use it as a statistic in posts.

## Her words (verbatim; usable as her own voice)
- "I didn't lack discipline. I lacked a system that respected female physiology under chronic stress."
- "With great difficulty, we were able to change the common perception that 'Healthy is not equal to tasty'."
- "You have to keep removing the friction between a person and the healthy choice." (Lo! Foods operating principle)
- "We wanted to keep the model lean and fluid from Day 1."
- "I learned why weight gain and regain happen so easily for women in midlife. That insight shapes everything I do."
- "The body isn't broken. It's protecting itself. My role is to remove the reasons it needs to."
- "I don't coach women to push harder. I coach them to understand what their body is signaling — and respond intelligently."
- "GLP-1 isn't the solution. But it can be a tool when used responsibly."
- "The real win is long-term stability — energy, confidence, and trust in their body."
