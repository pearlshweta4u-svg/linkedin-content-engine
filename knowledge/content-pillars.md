# Content pillars and weekday calendar

All times are Asia/Kolkata (IST). Posts go out Monday–Friday at 08:00. Nothing on Saturday or Sunday.

| Day | Category key | Objective | Main sources | Research |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | `personal_story` | Connection and trust | `story-bank-personal.md` | Not normally |
| Tue | `client_story` | Credibility | `case-bank-clients.md` (+ Fathom once it works) | When the lesson makes a factual claim |
| Wed | `educational` | Authority | `beliefs-and-methodology.md` + research | Yes, for any factual claim |
| Thu | `save_worthy` | Bookmarks and shares | Current topic (last 7–30 days) + method + cases | **Required**, deep research |
| Fri | `health_wellness` | Awareness, healthier decisions | Credible medical sources | **Required** |

## Topic pillars (rotate, track balance in the index)
1. **Thyroid & Hashimoto's**: conversion, antibodies, labs beyond TSH, medication timing, iodine.
2. **PCOS & insulin resistance**: fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, prediabetes window, cycles.
3. **Perimenopause**: the 40s transition, progesterone, sleep, cravings, heavy bleeds.
4. **Gut health**: acidity, constipation, infections, why "healthy" foods can backfire.
5. **Iron, B12, vitamin D**: the deficiency triad in Indian women; why levels won't rise.
6. **Strength, fat loss & body composition**: lifting at 40, steps, protein for vegetarians.
7. **Sleep, stress & cortisol**.
8. **Mental health & the emotional side**: fear of medication, guilt, privately managing symptoms.
9. **Women's health system & self-advocacy**: what to ask your doctor, "normal" reports.
10. **Founder & coach life**: building the practice, mistakes, honesty (Monday only, at most 1 in 4 Mondays).

Balance rules (enforced by `python -m engine gaps`):
- The same pillar no more than 2 times in any rolling 5 posts.
- Every pillar from 1 to 9 appears at least once every 4 weeks.
- A personal or client story can't repeat for 8 weeks. A topic angle can't repeat for 6 weeks.

## Thursday formats (rotate)
Checklist · lab panel "ask your doctor" list · mistakes to avoid · step-by-step · comparison table (as text) · do's and don'ts · "what X actually means" decoder · a recent study or guideline explained.

## Friday topic pool (start here, research before use)
Mental health · gut health · menstrual health · hormonal health · women's health · sleep · stress · nutrition · exercise · preventive screening. Prefer timely hooks: awareness months (e.g. September PCOS Awareness Month, January Thyroid Awareness Month, October Menopause Awareness Month / 18 Oct World Menopause Day, 14 Nov World Diabetes Day), new guidelines and major studies.

Allowed sources (in order): systematic reviews and peer-reviewed studies (PubMed), WHO, ICMR / Indian government health bodies, NIH/NHS/CDC, professional societies (Endocrine Society, ATA, Menopause Society, ESHRE PCOS guideline), established medical institutions (Mayo, Cleveland Clinic, Harvard Health). **Not** blogs, influencers or supplement sellers.
Every Friday and Thursday post states evidence strength honestly ("early research suggests…" vs "well established").
